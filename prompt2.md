2. **Regulatory Catalyst (Mandatory Compliance)**:
   - Bank Indonesia's BSPI 2030 (Blueprint Sistem Pembayaran Indonesia) framework mandates modern open payment infrastructure, high scalability, and microservices compliance.
3. **OpenWay (WAY4) Market Strength**:
   - Unrivaled market position in Tier-1 national infrastructure: BRI, Finnet, and ASBANDA/BPDNet.
4. **The Next Wave of Growth (Market Potential)**:
   - Commercial and mid-tier banks are under acute regulatory & cost pressure to migrate from rigid legacy platforms to compliant, cloud-native switching architectures.
